During my [doctoral research](https://doi.org/10.25673/35044), I worked on vector quasi-variational inequalities. These problems are mathematically challenging because they combine two difficult problem classes in one, set-valued fixed-point problems and vector variational inequalities, and their study requires a deep understanding of nonlinear functional analysis. My publications from this period cover existence theory, regularization methods, inverse problems and algorithms for these and related problems.

Today, my work and research focus on cryptography, a field that complements my earlier work in optimization and variational analysis.
