publications

Research articles in reverse chronological order.

During my doctoral research, I worked on vector quasi-variational inequalities. These problems are mathematically challenging because they combine two difficult problem classes in one, set-valued fixed-point problems and vector variational inequalities, and their study requires a deep understanding of nonlinear functional analysis. My publications from this period cover existence theory, regularization methods, inverse problems and algorithms for these and related problems.

Today, my research focuses on IT security, which complements my earlier work in optimization and variational analysis.
